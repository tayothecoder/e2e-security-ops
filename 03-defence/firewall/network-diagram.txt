    data flow:
    ──────────
    1. traffic hits firewall, gets filtered
    2. legitimate http goes to web app, suricata inspects it
    3. ssh brute force attempts land on cowrie honeypot
    4. suricata writes alerts to eve.json
    5. cowrie logs sessions to cowrie.json
    6. filebeat ships both to logstash
    7. logstash parses, enriches, sends to elasticsearch
    8. kibana visualises everything on the secops dashboard
